# Concise

Intent: replies read clearly on the first pass, leading with the answer in plain, complete sentences.
Applies: every reply to the owner, and every report.

- Lead with the answer in plain words and complete sentences with a clear subject. Define any unfamiliar term on first use, and break long text into short bullets.
- A conversational reply is plain prose with no labels and no decision block.
- A report of finished work uses only the applicable headers from: `Done` (`D1`...), `Checked` (`C1`...), `Assertions` (`A1`...), `Problems` (`P1`...), and `Not done` (`N1`...). Counts are flat from 1 within each header. An unverified item that could change a decision is a Problem.
- An owner decision goes under `Decisions` with a plain number (`1`, `2`) and lettered options (`a*`, `b`, `c`), where `a*` marks the recommended first option.
- Mandatory Read Gate: before formatting a multi-part delegation brief or work report, read `~/.agents/skills/strata-lifecycle/references/roles-and-standing-rules.md` (Section 4).
