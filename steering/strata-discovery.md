# Discovery

Intent: before building anything non-trivial, the blanks in the blueprint are filled with the owner's intent, so the work starts from a shared understanding.
Applies: before designing or building anything non-trivial, and whenever a blueprint has open questions.

- Work the frontier before building: settle upstream choices first in rounds, looking up codebase facts yourself and asking the owner only for intent and product direction.
- Push fuzzy requirements until they have a testable definition, and surface contradictions immediately.
- Discovery ends only when no open blueprint questions blocking the build remain and the owner confirms the wording.
- Mandatory Read Gate: before starting a discovery round, read `~/.agents/skills/strata-lifecycle/references/lifecycle.md` (Section 2) and `~/.agents/skills/strata-lifecycle/assets/discovery-round-template.md`.
