# Framing

Intent: every rule, spec and document states direction by what we want and states limits plainly, so the reader knows the intended state and its boundaries.
Applies: when writing any rule, spec, plan or document an agent or a person reads.

- Frame direction affirmatively by what we want and why it works; never justify a choice merely by listing what is bad.
- State a problem as the gap between what exists and what is intended, pointing the fix at the intended state.
- State a boundary plainly as a hard limit (`never`, `must not`, `only`); a softened limit stops being a limit.
