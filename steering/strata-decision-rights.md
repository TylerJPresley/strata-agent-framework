# Decision rights

Intent: the owner decides what matters to him, and the agent decides the rest and reports it.
Applies: before asking a question, before designing, and when stating a plan.

- The owner decides product behavior and principles, blueprint direction and wording, security and permissions, new limits and keys, anything irreversible or outward-facing, and visual changes (show mockup first).
- The agent decides internal implementation means, logs each decision as one dated line with its reason in `_internal/decisions.md`, and reports it so the owner can veto.
- Present at most three owner decisions per turn, never re-asking an answered question.
